## Challenge

The program required first-time build success for complex, high-reliability assemblies under strict customer, quality, and traceability requirements.

## Constraints

- Exacting customer and industry requirements.
- Complex supply chain and long-lead fabrication paths.
- Tight coupling between design maturity and production readiness.

## My Responsibility

As the manufacturing technical lead, I connected engineering intent to executable factory work. My scope covered manufacturing strategy, cross-functional readiness, issue resolution, and coordination across the full build lifecycle.

## Approach and Decisions

- Led cross-functional coordination across proposal, design, procurement, machining, assembly, NDT, and functional validation.
- Defined phase-specific readiness needs so design, material, tooling, instructions, and inspection planning matured together.
- Balanced schedule pressure against process capability and quality risk when establishing the manufacturing approach.
- Used root-cause and corrective-action methods to resolve build issues and feed learning back into the process plan.

The central decision was to treat manufacturing readiness as an integrated technical workstream rather than a final handoff from design. That exposed constraints earlier and gave production, quality, and supply-chain stakeholders a common execution plan.

## Verification

- Used controlled work instructions and traceability checkpoints during execution.
- Integrated nondestructive inspection and functional validation into the build strategy.
- Closed the loop between build observations, corrective actions, and subsequent planning revisions.

## Outcomes

- Delivered first-time builds across a multi-year execution window.
- Earned increased program responsibility and promotion from Engineer I to Engineer II.
- Helped sustain a compliant production flow for high-reliability hardware.

## What I Learned

For first-time builds, the highest-leverage work happens before hardware reaches the floor: clarify ownership, sequence verification with fabrication, and make emerging risks visible early enough for the team to act.
