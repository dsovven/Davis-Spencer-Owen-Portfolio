## Challenge

Research objective required an integrated sensing/stimulation PCB platform for Type II Diabetes work, combining hardware design and procurement constraints.

## Constraints

- Academic lab budget and lead-time limitations.
- Iterative prototype needs with evolving technical requirements.

## What Was Implemented

- Led concept research and technical option evaluation.
- Supported PCB design decisions and component selection.
- Managed procurement planning to keep prototype iteration moving.

## Verification

- Compared candidate technical approaches against evolving research requirements.
- Reviewed component availability and lead-time constraints alongside design choices.
- Maintained a structured path from research questions to prototype decisions.

## Outcomes

- Delivered a working development path for device prototyping.
- Established a structured design-procurement workflow for lab execution.

## What I Learned

In early research hardware, procurement is part of the design problem: component availability, iteration speed, and technical uncertainty must be evaluated together.
